# West marches economy

The closed economy of the west marches town: coin and goods are conserved, villagers and traders produce, buy and sell, and prices come out of what is on the shelves and who makes what. the owner wants swings, lags and rare long shortages to emerge from that, never to be scripted.

## Language

**Wave**:
A fixed sine rhythm laid over a price by a shop or an item. A wave is not a trend and carries no cause.
_Avoid_: season, cycle (for the sine kind)

**Moon rhythm**:
A producer's output rising and falling with a named moon (the eel farmer with the Red Lantern). A scheduled rhythm of supply, kept apart from trends.
_Avoid_: trend

**Trend**:
A price movement that persists for days or weeks because of what happens in the economy (stock, production, lag), not because a curve says so.
_Avoid_: drift, wave

**Chain**:
A causal link between goods: one good is an input of another or feeds what makes it, so a price move in the first shows up in the second after the time production takes (barley, then hens, then eggs). Never a coefficient or an imposed lag.
_Avoid_: follow, correlation

## Prices and producers

**Scarcity price**:
The price of a good made from how little of it is on the shelf, how little is in the whole village, and the price it had before. Dear when scarce, cheap when plentiful, never pulled back by a rule.
_Avoid_: drift, price pull

**Production lag**:
The time between a producer's decision, taken at the price of the day, and the goods arriving (planting to harvest, chick to laying hen). Lags are what make chains swing.
_Avoid_: delay coefficient

**Own use**:
A producer keeping goods for themselves instead of selling when the good is scarce and dear (the tailor wearing the only shirt).
_Avoid_: hoarding

**Stuck episode**:
A rare stretch of weeks in which a good stays at an extreme price because the actors' own behaviour keeps it there, until that same behaviour ends it. Never scripted and never permanent.
_Avoid_: regime, trap (unless named after its cause)

**Sticky price**:
A shopkeeper's habit of cutting a dear price only slowly once it has been high, so a price stays up for weeks after the shortage that raised it has passed. One cause of a stuck episode.
_Avoid_: ratchet, price floor

**Household**:
The village as buyers: the owners, staff and families of the shops and everyone unplayed, taken together. They are paid from what pools in the tills, eat the staples of the diet at the price of the day and spend only what they hold.
_Avoid_: demand, consumers

**Wholesale rate**:
The larger share of its price a shop pays for fresh staples, because it sells them on at once. It keeps a producer who buys inputs at shop prices out of the red.
_Avoid_: sell-back (that is the share paid for everything else)

**Working capital**:
The coin a producer needs in the purse to buy inputs before the goods are made and sold. A producer without it makes nothing.
_Avoid_: float, loan

## The steward

**Steward**:
Hedwig Dornbusch, the elf herb woman, who steers the village only by her own deeds and steps in only to prevent a disaster. She does not trade for profit and does not hoard.
_Avoid_: governor, market maker, trader (she is a trader in the code only)

**Disaster**:
A trouble that has lasted for weeks and would end a trade for good if nobody stepped in: the chickens dying out, a producer out of working capital, a staple gone from the village, a price stuck at an extreme until its producer gives up. Ordinary swings are not disasters.
_Avoid_: crisis, shock

**Standing order**:
A rule the steward wrote ahead that carries itself out every night once the trouble it names has lasted for its stated number of nights.
_Avoid_: script, policy

**Delver**:
The village's one adventurer-NPC, who comes back from the dungeon about every month with a few gold pieces of treasure. Dungeon treasure is how new coin enters the village. With ageing on he is a villager: the strongest adult in their prime, and the treasure follows his strength.
_Avoid_: hero, player character

**Match**:
The herb woman's hidden hand: two single adults of the same kind she wants to see wed. A matched pair weds far likelier each month. Nobody in the village learns of it.
_Avoid_: arranged marriage

**Coming of age**:
A villager born in play gets a 0-level character at eighteen, rolled with the abilities inherited from the parents; until then they are a child and cannot be taken over.

**Plot**:
One of the village's hundred small barley fields. Owned by a person, inherited by the spouse, sold at a price that follows barley. Sown, ripe after forty-one nights, rests twenty-nine.
_Avoid_: field (ambiguous with a form field in the web code), acre

**Light**:
What barley grows by: the moons' brightness over the twelve nights before a harvest. The rule lives only in fields.py and DESIGN.md 28.
_Avoid_: weather (that is the other factor, which barley no longer follows)

**Fat night**:
A night the eel ponds give five times their catch. Defined in fields.py and DESIGN.md 28 and nowhere the village can read.

**Teaching**:
The herb woman's third hidden hand (with matches and blocks): a child she names takes a trade she names at eighteen, most of the time.
_Avoid_: apprenticeship
